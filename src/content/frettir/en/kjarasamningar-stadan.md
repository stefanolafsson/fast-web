FAST does not yet have a collective agreement in force with Reykjavík University (RU). The board is currently working to secure a collective agreement for its members.

## Where things stand

The board of FAST has drawn up a draft collective agreement and is now in talks with BHM — the Confederation of University Graduates — and its lawyers to shape the final version. The aim is to secure members a fair collective agreement that addresses pay, holiday entitlement, sick-leave rights and other terms of employment.

## Next steps

Once the final version of the collective agreement is ready, it will be presented to FAST members and put to a vote. Members will thus have the opportunity to review the content of the agreement and vote on it before it takes effect.

## Where to turn

- More information about the terms covered by BHM's collective bargaining can be found on the [BHM website](https://www.bhm.is)
- Members can contact the [FAST board](mailto:fast@bhm.is) with questions about terms and rights and the status of the negotiations

Members will be kept informed of developments here on the website and by email.
