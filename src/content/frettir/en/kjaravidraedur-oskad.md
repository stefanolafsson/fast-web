The board of FAST has sent Reykjavík University (RU) a formal letter requesting a meeting with representatives of the university to open negotiations on a collective agreement for FAST members.

## Background

FAST does not yet have a collective agreement in force with RU. The board has been preparing for negotiations in cooperation with [BHM](https://www.bhm.is), and this letter is the next step in that process. Read more in [Collective agreement — the state of play](/en/news/kjarasamningar-stadan/).

## Next steps

Members will be kept informed of developments here on the website and by email once the university responds and talks begin.

Members can direct questions about terms and rights and the status of the negotiations to the [FAST board](mailto:fast@bhm.is).
