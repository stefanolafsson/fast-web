FAST — the Association of Academic Staff at Reykjavík University — is convening its annual general meeting on Friday 29 May at 11:30 in room V105.

## Agenda

The meeting will cover the standard annual general meeting business in accordance with the association's bylaws. The following will also be discussed:

- Changes to the association's bylaws
- The status of the collective agreement negotiations

## Standing for office

Members are encouraged to put themselves forward for positions of trust within the association.

A light lunch will be served.
